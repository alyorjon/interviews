# 🧪 Behavioral Interview (Mind) Interaktiv Quiz — Self-Test MCQ
### Ikki tilli qo'llanma / Bilingual Reference

> **Daraja / Level:** 🟢 Basic → 🟡 Intermediate → 🔴 Advanced
> **Format:** Har bir savolda 4 variant, faqat bittasi to'g'ri. Javoblarni oldindan ko'rmang — avval yeching! / Each question has 4 options, only one correct. Don't peek at the answer key — attempt first!
> **Javoblar:** Fayl oxirida / **Answers:** at the end of the file

## 🟢 Basic (1–10)

**1.** Xulq-atvor (behavioral) savollariga javob berishda tavsiya etilgan struktura qanday? / What structured method is recommended for answering behavioral questions?
A) STAR usuli — Vaziyat, Vazifa, Harakat, Natija / STAR method — Situation, Task, Action, Result
B) FAST usuli / FAST method
C) Faqat hikoya aytish, struktura shart emas / Just tell a story, no structure needed
D) SWOT tahlili / SWOT analysis

**2.** "Eng katta zaif tomoningiz" savoliga qanday javob berish tavsiya etiladi? / What's the recommended approach to "greatest weakness"?
A) Zaif tomoningiz yo'qligini ayting / Say you have no weaknesses
B) Halol bo'ling va faol ishlab kelayotgan real zaiflikni ko'rsating / Be honest and show a real weakness you're actively improving
C) Muvaffaqiyatni zaiflik sifatida ko'rsating (masalan "juda ko'p ishlayman") / Disguise a strength as weakness ("I work too hard")
D) Savoldan qochib, boshqa mavzuga o'ting / Avoid the question and change topic

**3.** Oxirgi ishdan ketish sababini so'rashganda nima qilish kerak? / When asked why you left your last job?
A) Oldingi ish beruvchi haqida batafsil shikoyat qiling / Complain in detail about the previous employer
B) Faqat maosh kam edi deng / Just say the salary was too low
C) Ijobiy va oldinga qarab gapiring, hech qachon badmouth qilmang / Be positive and forward-looking, never badmouth them
D) Sababni aytishdan bosh tortish / Refuse to answer

**4.** Muvaffaqiyatsizlik haqida gapirganda nimaga e'tibor qaratish kerak? / When discussing a failure, what should you emphasize?
A) Aybni jamoadoshlarga yoki tashqi omillarga yuklash / Shift blame to teammates or external factors
B) Muvaffaqiyatsizlikni umuman tilga olmaslik / Avoid mentioning any failure at all
C) Muammo hech qachon sodir bo'lmagandek gapirish / Pretend the problem never really happened
D) Mas'uliyatni o'z zimmangizga olish va undan nima o'rganganingizni ko'rsatish / Take accountability and show what you learned

**5.** Stressni qanday boshqarish haqida gapirganda nima yaxshi javob hisoblanadi? / What makes a strong answer about handling stress?
A) Aniq strategiyalar bilan misol keltirish (masalan, vazifalarni bo'lish, ustuvorlik) / Giving a concrete example with real strategies (breaking down tasks, prioritizing)
B) "Men doim xotirjammman" deyish, tafsilotsiz / Just saying "I stay calm" with no detail
C) Stress hech qachon bo'lmaganini aytish / Claiming you never feel stress
D) Bosim ostida ishlamasligingizni tan olish / Admitting you can't work under pressure

**6.** Muammoli hamkasb bilan ishlashni tasvirlashda qaysi yondashuv to'g'ri? / Which approach is right when describing working with a difficult coworker?
A) Ziddiyat va janjalni bo'rttirib ko'rsatish / Dramatize the conflict
B) Yetuklik va professionallik bilan, muammoni hamkorlikda hal qilganingizni ko'rsatish / Show maturity and professionalism, resolving it collaboratively
C) Hamkasbni suhbatda yomonlash / Badmouth the coworker in the interview
D) Menejerga shikoyat qilib, boshqa choralar ko'rmaganingizni aytish / Say you just complained to the manager and did nothing else

**7.** Konstruktiv tanqidni qabul qilishda eng yaxshi munosabat qanday? / What's the best attitude toward constructive criticism?
A) Mudofaaga o'tib, tanqidni rad etish / Get defensive and reject it
B) Tanqidni e'tiborsiz qoldirib, gapni o'zgartirish / Ignore it and change the subject
C) Ochiqlik bilan qabul qilib, harakatga aylantirish (masalan, jarayonni yaxshilash) / Welcome it openly and turn it into action (e.g., improving a process)
D) Tanqid qilgan hamkasbga o'pkalab yurish / Hold a grudge against the colleague who gave it

**8.** Ish tajribangizda "raqamlar ishlatish" maslahatiga misol qaysi? / Which example follows the "use numbers" tip from the Key Tips section?
A) "Men juda ko'p ishladim va yaxshi natijaga erishdim" / "I worked really hard and got a good result"
B) "Loyiha muvaffaqiyatli yakunlandi" / "The project was completed successfully"
C) "Jamoam mendan mamnun edi" / "My team was happy with me"
D) "Joylashtirish vaqtini 45 daqiqadan 8 daqiqaga tushirdim" / "I reduced deployment time from 45 to 8 minutes"

**9.** Muhim ma'lumotlarga kirish xatosini topganingizda (masalan, mijoz moliyaviy ma'lumotlari oshkor bo'lishi mumkin) nima qilish kerak degan yondashuv to'g'ri? / What's the recommended approach upon discovering a bug that could expose client financial data?
A) Darhol kattalarga xabar berish, hatto chiqarish kechiksa ham / Escalate it immediately, even if it delays the release
B) Chiqarishni kechiktirmaslik uchun jim turish / Stay silent to avoid delaying the release
C) Faqat o'zingiz tuzatib, hech kimga aytmaslik / Quietly fix it yourself and tell no one
D) Keyingi sprintgacha kutish / Wait until the next sprint to mention it

**10.** Suhbat oxirida "Bizga savollaringiz bormi?" deyilganda nima tavsiya etiladi? / What's recommended when asked "Do you have questions for us?" at the end?
A) "Yo'q, hammasi tushunarli" deb qisqa javob berish / Simply say "No, everything is clear"
B) Oldindan tayyorlangan 2–3 ta aqlli savol berish / Have 2-3 smart questions prepared in advance
C) Faqat maosh haqida so'rash / Only ask about salary
D) Suhbatdan intervyuerga savol berish noodob deb hisoblash / Consider it rude to ask the interviewer anything

## 🟡 Intermediate (11–20)

**11.** Menejer bilan kelishmasangiz, tavsiya etilgan yondashuv qanday? / When you disagree with your manager, what's the recommended approach?
A) Ochiq norozilik bildirmasdan, jim faqat o'zicha bajarish / Silently comply without voicing disagreement
B) Boshqa jamoadoshlarni o'z tomoningizga og'dirish / Rally other teammates to your side
C) Ma'lumotlar bilan nuqtai nazaringizni bildirish, keyin qarorga rioya qilib natijalarga gapirtirish / Present your perspective with data, then commit to the final decision and let results speak
D) Menejerning qarorini oshkora rad etish / Publicly reject the manager's decision

**12.** Ishlab chiqarishda xato (masalan, noto'g'ri migratsiya) qilganingizda qanday munosabat to'g'ri hisoblanadi? / When you make a production mistake (e.g., a bad migration), what's the right response?
A) Xatoni yashirish va hech kimga aytmaslik / Hide the mistake and tell no one
B) Xatoni boshqa jamoadoshga yuklash / Blame another teammate
C) Faqat kechirim so'rab, hech narsa o'zgartirmaslik / Just apologize and change nothing
D) Darhol orqaga qaytarish, shaffof xabar berish va oldini olish chorasini joriy etish / Roll back immediately, communicate transparently, and add a prevention measure

**13.** Past samarali jamoa a'zosi bilan ishlashda birinchi qadam nima bo'lishi kerak? / What should be the first step when dealing with an underperforming team member?
A) Asosiy sababni tushunish (ko'nikma bo'shlig'i, shaxsiy masala yoki noaniq kutish) / Understand the root cause (skill gap, personal issue, or unclear expectations)
B) Darhol ishdan bo'shatish / Fire them immediately
C) Boshqa jamoadoshlar oldida tanbeh berish / Reprimand them in front of the team
D) E'tibor bermay, ishni boshqasiga topshirish / Ignore it and reassign their work to someone else

**14.** Jamoada mas'uliyatni ta'minlash bo'yicha tavsiya etilgan tamoyil qaysi? / Which principle is recommended for ensuring accountability in a team?
A) Qattiq nazorat va doimiy kuzatuv / Strict surveillance and constant monitoring
B) Aniq vazifa egaligi va aybsiz (blame-free) retrospektivalar — tizimlar, nazorat emas / Clear task ownership and blame-free retrospectives — systems over surveillance
C) Xato qilganlarni ommaviy jazolash / Publicly punishing those who make mistakes
D) Har bir qadamni menejerga tasdiqlatish / Requiring manager approval for every single step

**15.** "Blameless Post-Mortem" madaniyati nimaga e'tibor qaratadi? / What does "Blameless Post-Mortem" culture focus on?
A) Xatoga sabab bo'lgan muhandisni aniqlash va jazolash / Identifying and punishing the engineer responsible
B) Voqeani hujjatlashtirmaslik / Not documenting the incident at all
C) Tizimli muammolarni (testlar yetishmasligi, CI/CD bo'shliqlari) tahlil qilish / Analyzing systemic issues (lack of tests, CI/CD gaps)
D) Faqat menejerlar orasida muhokama qilish / Discussing it only among managers

**16.** Ishda ziddiyatni hal qilishda tavsiya etilgan yondashuv qanday? / What's the recommended approach to resolving conflict at work?
A) Muammoni e'tiborsiz qoldirib, o'zi hal bo'lishini kutish / Ignore it and hope it resolves itself
B) Ziddiyatni kattalashtirib, boshqa bo'lim rahbariga shikoyat qilish / Escalate it dramatically to another department's manager
C) Ikkala tomonni ham ayblab, betaraf turish / Blame both sides and stay uninvolved
D) Erta murojaat qilish va shaxsga emas, muammoga e'tibor qaratish / Address it early and focus on the issue, not the person

**17.** Maosh kutishlari haqida so'rashganda tavsiya etilgan strategiya nima? / What's the recommended strategy when asked about salary expectations?
A) Bozor narxini o'rganib, diapazon berish va moslashuvchanlik ko'rsatish / Research market rate, give a range, and show flexibility
B) Raqam aytishdan butunlay bosh tortish / Refuse to give any number at all
C) Iloji boricha yuqori raqam aytib, muzokaraga o'rin qoldirmaslik / State an extremely high number with no room for negotiation
D) Hozirgi maoshingizdan kam bo'lsa ham rozi bo'lishingizni aytish / Say you'll accept anything, even less than your current salary

**18.** Kompaniyaga pul tejaganingiz haqida gapirganda nima muhim? / What's important when talking about saving a company money?
A) Faqat umumiy "ko'p pul tejadim" deb aytish / Just saying "I saved a lot of money" generally
B) Aniq raqam va o'lchov birligini qo'shish (masalan "$800/oyiga") / Attaching a concrete number and metric (e.g., "$800/month")
C) Buni jamoaviy yutuq deb, o'z hissangizni tilga olmaslik / Framing it purely as a team win without mentioning your role
D) Bu mavzuni suhbatda umuman ko'tarmaslik / Avoiding this topic in the interview altogether

**19.** "Kutilganidan ko'proq harakat qilish" (above and beyond) haqidagi javobda nimadan qochish kerak? / What should be avoided in an "above and beyond" answer?
A) Tashabbusni ko'rsatishdan / Showing initiative
B) Natijani o'lchashdan / Measuring the result
C) Fidokorlik (martyrdom) ohangidan — masalan, haddan tashqari charchoq va qurbonlikni ta'kidlash / A martyrdom tone — emphasizing exhaustion and sacrifice
D) Muammoni o'zingiz payqaganingizni aytishdan / Mentioning that you spotted the problem yourself

**20.** Ish-hayot balansi haqida so'rashganda qanday javob tavsiya etiladi? / What kind of answer is recommended for a work-life balance question?
A) "Men hech qachon dam olmayman" deb ishtiyoqni ko'rsatish / Claiming "I never take breaks" to show dedication
B) Bu savolni shaxsiy deb hisoblab javob bermaslik / Refusing to answer, calling it too personal
C) Ish soatlaringiz cheksiz ekanini ta'kidlash / Emphasizing that your working hours are unlimited
D) Halol javob berib, sog'lom chegaralar (masalan, aniq to'xtash vaqti) ko'rsatish / Being honest and showing healthy boundaries (e.g., set stop times)

## 🔴 Advanced (21–30)

**21.** Noaniq talablar bilan ishlashda tavsiya etilgan yondashuv qanday? / What's the recommended approach when facing ambiguous requirements?
A) Bir nechta talqin variantini tayyorlab, eng ehtimolisi bo'yicha MVP qurib, fikr-mulohaza asosida takomillashtirish / Draft multiple interpretations, build the most likely one as an MVP, then iterate on feedback
B) Aniqlik kelmaguncha ishni to'xtatib turish / Stop all work until clarity arrives
C) O'zingiz bilganingizcha, hech kim bilan maslahatlashmasdan davom etish / Proceed on your own assumptions without consulting anyone
D) Vazifani rad etish, chunki talablar aniq emas / Reject the task because requirements aren't clear

**22.** Loyihaning oxirgi daqiqasida talablar o'zgarganda (scope creep) nima qilish tavsiya etiladi? / What's recommended when last-minute scope changes hit a project?
A) Jim ravishda qo'shimcha ishni qabul qilib, hech narsa demaslik / Silently absorb the extra work without saying anything
B) Ta'sirni baholab (vaqt, murakkablik) va variantlarni aniq muloqot qilish (masalan, nimadir tashlab ketiladi yoki muddat suriladi) / Assess the impact and clearly communicate trade-offs (e.g., something gets dropped or the deadline shifts)
C) O'zgarishni butunlay rad etish / Flatly refuse the change
D) Boshqa jamoa a'zolariga aytmasdan mustaqil qaror qabul qilish / Decide unilaterally without informing the team

**23.** Kimnidir ishontirish (persuasion) kerak bo'lganda samarali usul qanday? / What's an effective approach to persuading someone?
A) Faqat obro'-e'tiboringizga tayanish / Relying solely on your authority/seniority
B) Qaror qabul qiluvchini bosim ostiga olish / Pressuring the decision-maker
C) Ma'lumotlar (masalan, prototip va o'lchovlar) bilan hamdardlikni birlashtirish / Combining data (e.g., a proof of concept with measurements) with empathy
D) Muqobil fikrlarni butunlay e'tiborsiz qoldirish / Ignoring the other person's concerns entirely

**24.** AI (Copilot) yordamida yozilgan kodni ko'rib chiqishda qaysi tamoyil tavsiya etiladi? / Which principle is recommended when reviewing AI (Copilot)-assisted code?
A) AI kodini har doim tekshirmasdan birlashtirish (merge) mumkin / AI-generated code can always be merged without review
B) AI kodi hech qachon xato qilmaydi / AI-generated code never contains bugs
C) Faqat linter yetarli, testlar shart emas / A linter alone is enough, tests aren't necessary
D) Mas'uliyat muhandisda qoladi — avtomatik testlar va chekka holatlarni tekshirish majburiy / Accountability stays with the engineer — automated tests and edge-case verification are mandatory

**25.** Texnik qarz (technical debt) va yangi funksiyalar o'rtasida balans qanday saqlanadi? / How is the balance between technical debt and new features maintained?
A) Har bir sprint sig'imining bir qismini (masalan, 20%) refaktoring va arxitektura yaxshilashga ajratish / Allocating a portion of each sprint's capacity (e.g., 20%) to refactoring and architectural improvements
B) Texnik qarzni butunlay e'tiborsiz qoldirish, faqat funksiyalarga e'tibor / Ignoring technical debt entirely and focusing only on features
C) Faqat mahsulot butunlay ishlamay qolgandan keyin qarzni to'lash / Only paying down debt after the product completely breaks
D) Texnik qarzni jamoaga aytmaslik / Not mentioning technical debt to the team at all

**26.** "Kompaniya haqida nima bilasiz?" savoliga yaxshi javob qanday tayyorlanadi? / How should a strong answer to "What do you know about our company?" be prepared?
A) Faqat kompaniya nomini eslash / Just mentioning the company's name
B) Chinakam tadqiq qilib, aniq mahsulotlar, qadriyatlar yoki so'nggi yangiliklarni tilga olish / Genuinely researching and citing specific products, values, or recent news
C) "Yaxshi kompaniya ekan" deb umumiy gapirish / Giving a generic "seems like a good company" response
D) Kompaniya haqida hech narsa bilmasligingizni tan olish / Admitting you know nothing about the company

**27.** Manfaatdor tomonlar (stakeholders) bilan ishonch qurishda tavsiya etilgan yondashuv qanday? / What's the recommended approach to building trust with stakeholders?
A) Faqat inqiroz bo'lganda aloqaga chiqish / Only reaching out during a crisis
B) Ular so'ragunicha hech qanday ma'lumot bermaslik / Withholding information until they explicitly ask
C) Muntazam, proaktiv yangilanishlar yuborish — hech kim so'ramasdan oldin / Sending regular, proactive updates — before anyone has to ask
D) Faqat yaxshi yangiliklarni ulashish, muammolarni yashirish / Sharing only good news and hiding problems

**28.** Yuqori bosimli vaziyatda (masalan, jonli ishlab chiqarish uzilishi) tavsiya etilgan reaksiya qanday? / What's the recommended response protocol during a high-pressure situation (e.g., a live production outage)?
A) Darhol tasodifiy tuzatishlar sinab ko'rish / Immediately trying random fixes
B) Hech kimga xabar bermay, yolg'iz hal qilishga urinish / Trying to fix it alone without telling anyone
C) Vaziyat o'zi hal bo'lishini kutish / Waiting for the situation to resolve itself
D) Qisqa vaqt baholash uchun to'xtash, holat haqida xabar berish, keyin muammoni tizimli hal qilish / Pausing briefly to assess, communicating status, then working the problem systematically

**29.** Xilma-xillik va kiritish (diversity & inclusion)ga qanday yondashuv tavsiya etiladi? / What approach to diversity and inclusion is recommended?
A) Yig'ilishlarda barcha ovozlar eshitilishini faol ta'minlash (masalan, jimroq jamoadoshlarga murojaat qilish) / Actively ensuring all voices are heard in meetings (e.g., specifically calling on quieter teammates)
B) Passiv qo'llab-quvvatlash, aniq harakatsiz / Passive support with no concrete action
C) Bu mavzuni ishda muhokama qilmaslik / Avoiding the topic at work altogether
D) Faqat rasmiy siyosatlarga tayanish, shaxsiy harakat qilmaslik / Relying only on formal policy, taking no personal action

**30.** Nima uchun "Biz" o'rniga "Men" deyish tavsiya etiladi intervyu javoblarida? / Why is it recommended to say "I" instead of "we" in interview answers?
A) Jamoa ishini kamsitish uchun / To belittle teamwork
B) O'z shaxsiy hissangizni aniq ko'rsatish uchun / To clearly own and highlight your individual contribution
C) Chunki "biz" grammatik jihatdan noto'g'ri / Because "we" is grammatically incorrect
D) Intervyuerlar "biz" so'zini yoqtirmaydi / Because interviewers dislike the word "we"
